Questions.

What is Sunday's Bloody Mary Mix?

Sunday's is a small-batch Bloody Mary mix sold in 32 oz glass jars in three heat levels: Spicy, Traditional and Mild. It won Platinum at the Drunken Tomato Awards three years running (2022, 2023 and 2024) and the People's Choice award at the 2026 NYC Bloody Mary Festival. You pour it over ice, add vodka if you want it, and garnish.

How spicy is each flavor?

Mild is savory with almost no burn. Traditional has the classic Bloody Mary kick. Spicy builds as you drink it without turning sharp; one customer described it as “not too hot, or too sharp.” If you order the hot wings on purpose, start with Spicy. If you're pouring for a mixed crowd, Traditional is the safe pick.

How many drinks does one jar make?

About eight. Each jar holds 32 oz, and a standard Bloody Mary uses roughly 4 oz of mix over ice. At $15.99 a jar, that works out to about $2 a drink before vodka and garnish. If you pour 6 oz into pint glasses, expect about five drinks per jar.

How do you make a Bloody Mary with Sunday's?

Rim a glass with cracked pepper and celery salt, fill it with ice, shake the jar, and pour about 4 oz of mix. Add 1.5 oz of vodka if you want alcohol, stir, and garnish with celery, olives, bacon or whatever you like. Nothing else is needed, because the seasoning is already in the mix.

How should I store it, and how long does it keep?

Shake well, refrigerate after opening and serve chilled. Those instructions are printed on every jar. (Details to be confirmed.)

Is it good without alcohol?

Yes. Pour it over ice, garnish it properly, and you have a very good Virgin Mary. Customers also cook with it; one reviewer added it to eggs for a Bloody Mary breakfast pizza and reported no leftovers.
